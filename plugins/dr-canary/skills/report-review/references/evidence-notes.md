# Read specialized evidence

- Read only the section relevant to the user's question.
- These notes interpret recorded customer evidence; they do not authorize a new test, alter a finding or replace missing evidence.

## User experience

- The shopper uses a site tool as a customer would.
- A step is confirmed only where the following page or returned record shows it succeeded.
- A challenge that prevented verification is unverified, not automatically a defect.
- Describe the visible symptom before suggesting a cause.

### Browser-view comparison

- A browser-view comparison needs equivalent actions and success conditions in the actual records.
- Viewport names or site-level pass/fail labels alone do not prove the same step ran in both.
- Preserve which side was not checked.

## Inquiry and follow-up

### Read the record

- Use `list_assignment_runs` for the recorded inquiry selectors and `get_inquiry` for the exact inquiry and replies.
- Preserve the original question, captured email/text order, current versus earlier review and delivery states.

### Separate receipt, response and answer

- A booking link is not necessarily an answer to the customer's specific question.
- “No reply captured” describes the available record; it does not prove there was no response through every channel or no activity in the business's CRM.

### Calls and identity

- The inquiry identifies itself as an automated Dr. Canary site test.
- The bird places no calls and sees only that a call came in.
- Do not invent a call transcript or infer a CRM outcome.

## Cookie consent

- The comparison concerns what the browser requested and stored on arrival, after rejection, after acceptance, after withdrawal and on a return visit.
- Describe only the stages actually evidenced.

### Read each stage

- “Still fires” means a request began after the recorded choice; pre-choice activity does not establish it.
- Closing a banner is a dismissal unless the evidence shows the site treated it as a choice. A selection covering some purposes is partial.
- Report a missing way to reopen choices as missing, without inventing a completed withdrawal.
- A cookie snapshot establishes presence or change, not the time it was written.
- Identify analytics, advertising or another purpose only where supported. A missing event or stage remains unresolved.

### Limits

- Consent evidence is not legal advice, a compliance certificate or a determination of which law applies.

## Report and capture availability

### Captures and links

- Use exact report selectors and returned application/evidence links.
- Captures can be null or unavailable, and a link-only oversized result omits the body.
- Do not manufacture a screenshot or a passing verdict from either state.

### Dates and repair

- Label an issued date, a check time or a report caption as such.
- None supplies an unreturned publication timestamp.
- A later missing finding proves repair only when the later evidence also establishes the original task was tried again and succeeded.
